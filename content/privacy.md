# Privacy Policy — LM18 Construction Employee App

**Effective Date:** August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **LM18 Construction** ("the Company," "we," "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal data when you use the **LM18 Construction Employee App** ("the App"). It is issued in compliance with **Republic Act No. 10173 (the "Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012," or "DPA")** and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and complements the **Terms of Service**.

By installing, accessing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the processing of your personal data described herein.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect and process only data necessary for the legitimate purposes described in this Policy:

| Category | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| Identity | Full name, employee ID, position, branch/site, photograph (selfies) |
| Contact | Contact details provided during onboarding |
| Attendance | Clock-in/out times, session history, buddy time-in records, late/missed-timeout records |
| Location | GPS coordinates and reverse-geocoded addresses collected during active shifts |
| Device | Device model, unique device identifier, battery level, app interactions |
| Payroll | Hours worked, overtime, hourly rate, payslip snapshots, statutory deductions |
| Security | Tamper-detection reports, mock-location alerts, location-interruption and app-interruption logs |

---

## 3. How and When We Collect Data

We collect data:

- **Directly from you** — information you provide or confirm during onboarding and when using the App.
- **Automatically from your device** — when you use the App, including selfies you capture for time records, your device's location during active shifts, device identifiers, and technical logs.
- **From authorized co-workers** — through the "Buddy Time In" feature, where an authorized co-worker captures and records your time-in using the App.

## 4. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

We process your personal data for the following purposes and legal bases:

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
| :--- | :--- |
| Attendance and time tracking | Performance of the employment contract; legitimate interest |
| Payroll and benefits computation | Performance of the employment contract; legal obligation |
| Compliance with labor, tax, and regulatory obligations | Legal obligation |
| Verifying identity through selfies and device records | Performance of the employment contract; legitimate interest |

## 5. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do **not** intentionally collect or process:

- Your browsing history, messages, calls, contacts, or other content unrelated to the App.
- Health data, biometric identifiers used for identification beyond the verification selfies described above, or other sensitive personal information, unless required by law or with your explicit consent.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes stated in this Policy or as required or permitted by applicable law (including tax and records-retention requirements).
- Once personal data is no longer necessary, it is securely disposed of or anonymized in a manner that prevents further processing, unauthorized access, or disclosure.

---

## 7. Security Measures

The Company implements organizational, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction, consistent with the DPA and the National Privacy Commission's security standards. These include:

- Access controls and restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.
- Secure, access-controlled cloud databases managed by the Company or its authorized service providers.
- Device- and app-level protections and tamper-detection controls.
- Periodic review and monitoring of access and processing activities.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Disclosure and Sharing of Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose your personal data only:

- **Within the Company** — to HR, payroll, and authorized management personnel on a need-to-know basis.
- **To service providers** — cloud database, image-storage, and IT providers bound by data-processing agreements that require them to protect your data in accordance with the DPA.
- **To government authorities** — where required by law, regulation, or lawful request (e.g., tax authorities, courts, the National Privacy Commission).
- **In connection with internal investigations or legal proceedings** — where lawfully required or permitted.

### Cross-Border Transfers

Some of our service providers may be located outside the Philippines. Where personal data is transferred abroad, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place as required by the DPA, and by using the App you consent to such processing and transfer.

## 9. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the DPA, you have the right to:

- **Be informed** whether and how your personal data is being processed.
- **Object** to the processing of your personal data on legitimate grounds.
- **Access** your personal data and request information about its processing.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
- **Suspend or withdraw** your consent where consent is the lawful basis for processing, subject to other applicable legal bases.
- **Request erasure or blocking** of personal data where its processing is unlawful, no longer necessary, or where you validly withdrew consent, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **Receive a copy** of your personal data in a commonly used format where feasible.
- **File a complaint** with the **National Privacy Commission** if you believe your rights have been violated.
